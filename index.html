What you are looking at

Look directly at any part of the rocket to read what it is and why it is built that way.

The Vehicle Analysis board is live. Watch the Δv figure as you add parts: two stages alone give about 13 900 m/s, and the eight-tonne telescope takes roughly 3 000 m/s of that away. Reaching orbit costs about 9 400 m/s, so the margin bar is what tells you whether this vehicle can fly the mission.
